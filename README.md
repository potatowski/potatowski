## 🌟 About me

Backend engineer at **Pigz**, a Brazilian restaurant management and point-of-sale platform. I've been building software since 2021, I've delivered 20+ projects along the way, and I'm pursuing a degree in Systems Analysis and Development.

I like owning problems end to end: from the database and the order flow to the conversation with product, support and vendors.

## 🚀 What I do at Pigz

- **Backend for almost every Pigz product** (Ficha, Comanda, Mesa, Kiosk, Partner, App, Ingressos and Gestão, desktop and mobile), growing with the platform from ~30 thousand to **50+ million orders**
- **Rewrote the order service twice**, the core every product goes through: in 2022 from a single service to order handlers per order type, and in 2025 to DTOs + factories
- **WhatsApp AI agent** (FuncionárIA Atendente) that serves customers and places real orders
- **Database administration and CI/CD** infrastructure
- **Lead a team of 14 people** and take part in technical decisions across every Pigz product
